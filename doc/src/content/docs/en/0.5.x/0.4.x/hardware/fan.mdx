An AUTD3 device has a fan with three modes: Auto / Off / On.

* Auto: A temperature-monitoring IC monitors the temperature and automatically starts the fan when it exceeds a certain temperature.
* Off: The fan is always off.
* On: The fan is always on.

The mode is switched with the jumper switch next to the fan.
It is a little hard to tell, but as shown in the figure below, shorting the fan side gives Auto, the middle gives Off, and the right side gives On.

<Figure src="img/hardware/fan.jpg" alt="Jumper switch for AUTD fan control" />

In Auto mode, the fan can be forcibly started by sending [`ForceFan`](/autd3-sdk/en/0.5.x/0.4.x/api/command/force-fan/).

:::note
In Auto mode, the fan cannot be forcibly turned off.
:::
