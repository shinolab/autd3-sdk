`Simulator` is a sound field simulator that visualizes the AUTD3 output sound field in a browser.
Even without real hardware, you can check the sound field generated from the data sent by the client.

## Launching

`Simulator` is bundled with the GUI console `autd3-console`.

Download `autd3-console` from the [Release page](https://github.com/shinolab/autd3-sdk/releases) on GitHub, launch it, and open the "Simulator" tab at the top.
`Simulator` can be launched from the tab.

## Connecting

Since `Simulator` internally launches a `RemoteServer`, the client connects to it with [`RemoteLink`](/autd3-sdk/en/0.4.x/api/link/remote/).
The connection target is `127.0.0.1:<link-port>` (default is 8080).

On connection, the simulator receives the client's `Geometry`, from which it determines the number of devices and their layout.
