An AUTD3 device consists of 249 transducers per unit\[^fn\_asm].
Each transducer is assigned the index shown below.

<Figure src="img/hardware/autd_trans_idx.jpg" alt="Photo of the AUTD3 surface with transducer indices" />

AUTD3 can be extended by connecting multiple units in a daisy chain, and the SDK can specify the phase / intensity of every transducer individually.
When connecting multiple units, connect the PC to the EtherCAT In of the first AUTD3 (see the figure below), and then connect the EtherCAT Out of the n-th AUTD3 to the EtherCAT In of the (n+1)-th AUTD3.

<Figure src="img/hardware/autd_back.jpg" alt="Photo of the AUTD3 rear" />

## Coordinate System

The coordinate system of AUTD3 is right-handed, and the center of the 0th transducer is the origin.
The x axis is the long-axis direction, i.e. the 0 → 17 direction, and the y axis is the 0 → 18 direction.
The unit of distance is mm.

## Dimensions

The transducers are arranged at intervals of <Math tex="10.16\,\mathrm{mm}" />, and the size including the board is <Math tex="192\,\mathrm{mm} \times 151.4\,\mathrm{mm}" />.

<Figure src="img/hardware/transducers_array.jpg" alt="Dimension diagram of the AUTD3 device" />

## Power Supply

The AUTD3 power supply uses a <Math tex="24\,\mathrm{V}" /> DC power source.
Power supplies can be interconnected, and you may use any of the three power connectors.
The power connector on the device side uses Molex 5566-02A.

:::note
AUTD3 consumes up to <Math tex="2\,\mathrm{A}" /> of current per device.
Take care about the maximum output current of the power supply.
:::

\[^fn\_asm]: Three transducers are removed from <Math tex="18 \times 14 = 252" /> for assembly. The screw holes were deliberately placed at these positions to make the gap as small as possible when multiple units are arranged side by side.
